Neon Decade — 80s Word Search

Thanks for grabbing this puzzle collection! It plays right in your web browser — no app store, no install. Here's how to get going.

1. Unzip the download

Your download is a ZIP file. Unzip / extract it first — most devices do this automatically when you tap or double-click the ZIP.

2. Open the puzzle file

Inside the unzipped folder, open index.html — that single file is the whole puzzle collection.

  • Windows / Mac: double-click index.html. It opens in your default browser.
  • Android phone or tablet: open the Files app, find index.html in the unzipped folder, and tap it to open in Chrome.
  • iPhone / iPad: due to Apple's restrictions on locally opened files, this file may open in a limited "Quick Look" preview instead of a full browser tab, and progress may not save. For the best experience on iPhone/iPad, we recommend using it on a Windows, Mac, or Android device.

3. Play

  • Pick a theme, then pick a puzzle inside it.
  • Two ways to select a word: drag across it from start to finish, or tap its first letter and then tap its last letter. Tap the starting letter again to cancel a selection.
  • Find every word on the list to clear the puzzle. Stuck? Reset puzzle clears your progress on that one puzzle so you can start it over, and Reveal all words shows the answers.
  • Clear all 50 puzzles to unlock the Bonus Zone: 8 extra puzzles, a secret finale puzzle, and an 80s trivia quiz.

Your progress

Progress saves automatically in your browser on that device — including puzzles you've started but not finished. If you switch browsers or devices, you'll start fresh there — your puzzles are still exactly the same, though.

Need help?

If something won't open, make sure the ZIP is fully extracted first (not opened directly from inside the ZIP), and try a different browser (Chrome or Edge work best).
