Quiet Season — 200 Puzzles for a Calm Mind

This collection plays right in your web browser — no app store, nothing to install. Here's everything you need to know.

What's inside

  • 100 Sudoku puzzles — Easy, Normal, and Hard.
  • 100 Word Search puzzles across ten cozy, nostalgic themes.
  • A Bonus Page — Daily Wisdom and Witty Monologues, open any time, nothing to unlock.

Playing Sudoku

Tap an empty square to select it, then tap a number to fill it in. Use Check to see if anything needs a second look, Reset to start the puzzle over, or Reveal to see the answer.

Playing Word Search

Drag your finger or mouse across a word from start to finish — or tap its first letter, then tap its last letter. Tap the same letter twice to cancel a selection. Reset puzzle and Reveal all words are there if you'd like them.

Your progress

Everything saves automatically in your browser as you go, including puzzles you've only half finished. Just close the page and come back whenever you like.

Adding it to your Home Screen (optional)

On iPhone or iPad (Safari): tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), then scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen." It will then open like a regular app icon, full screen, with no browser bar.

On Android (Chrome): tap the three-dot menu in the top right, then tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app" (Chrome sometimes offers this automatically as a small banner too).

On Windows or Mac: this step is entirely optional — the puzzles work perfectly well as a regular browser tab or bookmark, no installation needed.

Need help?

If a page doesn't load, try refreshing, or open the link in a different browser (Chrome or Safari work best).
